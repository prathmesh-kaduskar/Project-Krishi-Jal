KrishiJal is an innovative agricultural solutions brand dedicated to improving soil health and enhancing crop productivity through science-backed, eco-friendly products. Designed for farmers who seek better yields with sustainable farming practices, KrishiJal offers advanced soil conditioning solutions that strengthen plant growth from the roots up.

Our flagship Humic + Fulvic Soil Conditioner is enriched with 70% Potassium Humate and 25% Fulvic Acid, providing essential nutrients that improve soil fertility, increase water retention, promote stronger root development, and enhance nutrient absorption. Suitable for all crops, KrishiJal's water-soluble capsule technology ensures easy application, consistent results, and maximum convenience.

At KrishiJal, we believe that healthy soil is the foundation of a prosperous harvest. Every product is developed with a focus on efficiency, quality, and environmental responsibility, helping farmers achieve higher yields while nurturing the land for future generations.
