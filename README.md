## 🌟 Overview

**Krishi-Jal** (*"स्वस्थ मिट्टी, बेहतर फसल, खुशहाल किसान"*) is an agricultural web platform built to provide advanced soil conditioning solutions. It empowers farmers with tools and eco-friendly products that strengthen plant growth from the roots up, boosting overall yields while maintaining environmental responsibility.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Responsive Header & Navigation:** Clean, modern navbar with quick anchors to Home, Products, About, and Buy Now sections.
- **Flagship Product Showcase:** Highlights high-efficiency soil conditioning technology with clear specifications.
- **Brand Story / About Us:** Detailed breakdown of Krishi-Jal's mission to enhance soil fertility, increase water retention, and promote robust root development.
- **Contact & Footer Integration:** Direct access to customer support, email (`krishijal@gmail.com`), location info (Mumbai, India), and social media handles.

---

## 🧪 Our Flagship Product

- **Product Name:** Krishi-Jal Soil Conditioner
- **Composition:** 70% Potassium Humate + 25% Fulvic Acid
- **Key Benefits:**
  - Improves soil fertility and structure.
  - Increases water retention capacity.
  - Promotes stronger root development and deeper nutrient absorption.
  - Water-soluble formula ensuring easy application and consistent results for all crop types.
